# Antecedent-wetness and local-stage evidence review

## Purpose

This review identifies evidence that could improve the experimental Maubin flood hindcast without manufacturing a local gauge record or weakening the chronological holdout. It follows the v8 non-promotion decision: a distant GTSM coastal proxy is not treated as a substitute for a Maubin water-level observation.

## Findings

| Evidence pathway | Verified coverage or content | Current eligibility decision |
|---|---|---|
| CEMS GloFAS historical reanalysis | The official CEMS catalogue exposes daily global river discharge, root-zone soil-wetness index, and total runoff water equivalent at 0.05° from 1979 to near real time. | Promising candidate source. It requires a separately reproducible authorised EWDS retrieval and extraction at a hydrologically defensible Maubin/upstream grid cell before use. |
| Nyaungdon/Panhlaing water-level study | The peer-reviewed study reports annual highest Panhlaing River water level at Nyaungdon for 1985–2016, but describes hydrological observations as limited. Its daily rainfall and discharge inputs for 2011, 2015, and 2016 were supplied by local government offices. | Not an immediately reproducible 2002–2018 daily predictor. It supports the importance of local-stage information but does not provide a complete machine-readable series for the current common holdout. |
| Existing native GFD event labels | Seventeen historical Maubin events already have pixel-derived labels with permanent water excluded. | Eligible for strictly prior-event, time-causal research features only. All 2018 labels remain excluded from held-out feature construction. |

## Resulting experiment order

The project will first evaluate model-capacity and strictly prior-event historical-susceptibility variants using the unchanged verified v7 data. No candidate will be promoted unless it improves the validation selection criterion and provides satisfactory common 2018 holdout performance. A future antecedent-wetness candidate requires successful CEMS/EWDS access, full coverage, documented grid selection, and the same one-day pre-event cutoff.
