# Candidate hydrologic predictors — source assessment

| Predictor family | Candidate source | Verified coverage and resolution | Use in candidate model | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Upstream river discharge, soil wetness, and runoff | [Copernicus CEMS GloFAS historical data](https://ewds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/cems-glofas-historical) | Global daily gridded hydrological time series, 0.05° resolution, from 1979 to near real time. | Extract current and lagged values at appropriate upstream river pixels; use discharge and wetness as non-local forcing proxies. | Modelled reanalysis, not a Maubin gauge observation; spatial alignment and leakage-safe lagging are required. |
| Tide elevation | [AVISO FES2022](https://www.aviso.altimetry.fr/en/data/products/auxiliary-products/global-tide-fes.html) | Global tide amplitude and phase grids at 1/30° resolution for 34 tidal components. | Derive maximum tide, tidal range, and high-tide timing for each historical flood event. | Coastal-ocean model proxy; it cannot substitute for a local river-stage or surge gauge. |
| Local gauge evidence | Myanmar Department of Meteorology and Hydrology public forecasts and Maubin irrigation references | Search evidence indicates Maubin water-level and local tide observations exist, but an open retrospective data service has not yet been verified. | Use only if a reproducible historical station archive can be obtained and its datum/quality metadata are documented. | Do not interpolate or manufacture missing gauge history. |
| Flood labels | [Official Global Flood Database storage archive](https://github.com/cloudtostreet/MODIS_GlobalFloodDatabase) | Seventeen Maubin-relevant event archives were resolved and downloaded from the public `gfd_v1_4` bucket. Each GeoTIFF provides native flood and permanent-water bands. | Calculate each 500 m terrain-cell target from GFD flooded pixels after excluding permanent water. | The data are satellite-derived, event-scale labels rather than field observations; cloud and mapping limitations remain. |
| Tide and surge | [Global Tide and Surge Reanalysis](https://research.vu.nl/en/datasets/daily-maxima-of-total-water-levels-from-the-global-tide-and-surge/) | Global daily tide-and-surge reanalysis covers 1979–2014, while the full Maubin held-out interval continues into 2018. | A candidate coastal predictor can be evaluated only over its common coverage period. | It cannot support a consistent 2018 holdout without an equivalent later data source, so tide is not silently inserted into v7. |
| Tide and surge | [GTSM-ERA5-E](https://zenodo.org/records/10671284) | CC-BY global daily-maximum water-level reanalysis with 1950–2022 coverage; the downloadable archive is 667.9 MB and includes the 2018 holdout period. | Extract the nearest coastal model node and calculate pre-event maximum, range, and surge features only from dates before each event. | Modelled coastal water level rather than a Maubin tide-gauge observation; node distance, vertical reference, and calendar completeness must be checked before use. |
| Satellite river stage | [DAHITI](https://dahiti.dgfi.tum.de/en/products/water-level-altimetry/) | Multi-mission altimetry database with downloadable ASCII, NetCDF, CSV, and JSON series; access requires short registration and sampling interval depends on satellite tracks. | Use only if a virtual station near Maubin or a hydrologically defensible upstream reach has sufficient historical overlap with labelled events. | Not daily gauge data; coverage may be irregular and water-level datum must be verified before combining with other predictors. |

## Next-stage acquisition quality checks

The public DAHITI catalogue confirms two relevant named virtual stations, but neither is currently eligible as a v8 predictor. The **Irrawaddy River** station (`9467`) is at **23.1111° N, 95.9747° E**, far upstream of Maubin, and its public chart indicates sparse multi-mission observations ending in 2016; the downloadable data require account registration. The **Ayeyarwady River** station (`9453`) is at **19.7350° N, 95.1508° E**, downstream of the delta head but still not a Maubin-local gauge, and its public chart also ends in 2016. Neither covers the 2018 held-out events, so neither will be inserted into the model or treated as local stage observations.

The public **GTSM-ERA5-E v1** archive was downloaded and inspected. Although the source record describes 1950–2022 daily maxima, the supplied archive contains annual files for 1950–1979 and 2019–2022 only; its 1980–2018 event-period files are absent. The newer GTSM record provides statistics only and points users to the C3S Climate Data Store for full 1979–2018 time series. A C3S account/API credential is therefore required before the tide-and-surge data can be extracted reproducibly for the full common holdout. FES2022 is available locally as a modelled tidal-constituent atlas, but it is not used as a substitute for total coastal water level or surge without a validated Maubin-specific transformation.

## Authorised GTSM acquisition outcome

After the required C3S licence was accepted, GTSM-ERA5-E v3 reanalysis daily-maximum source archives were acquired for each of the 17 Maubin historical event windows and their preceding three days. The nearest valid modelled coastal node is at **16.1870° N, 95.8450° E**, 0.57695° from the Maubin reference point. Its daily total-water-level and storm-surge-residual values are retained as coastal forcing proxies, not as a Maubin local gauge or independent tide observation. A leakage-safe v8 experiment was run, but its validation F1, PR-AUC, and ROC-AUC did not surpass the production v7 candidate. The v8 coastal proxy is consequently documented but not deployed.

Dynamic predictors are evaluated with dates strictly earlier than each labelled observation to prevent target leakage. The v7 experiment includes verified ERA5 rainfall lags, GloFAS discharge lags, and OpenStreetMap drainage or levee distances. A tide feature remains explicitly deferred until a validated series spanning the complete 2002–2018 evaluation window is acquired.
